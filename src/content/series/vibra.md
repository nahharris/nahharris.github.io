Vibra is my exploration of what a programming language can be when agents are central to how software is written and maintained. Its syntax, type and effect systems, tooling, and runtime are being designed together.

The current work is specification-first. The repository describes the v1 target and its implementation roadmap; it does not yet offer a supported compiler or release. I will share the story here as it develops.
