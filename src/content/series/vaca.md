**Vaca is sunset.** I am no longer developing it as an active language project. Its repository remains available as a record of what I tried and learned.

Vaca began as a playground for a larger idea and became my first working language implementation: a Rust-built, dynamically typed language with a Lisp-inspired syntax. A later reboot explored EDN-derived syntax and a new interpreter. These posts follow the experiment and the decision to bring Vaca to a close as I move toward Vibra.
